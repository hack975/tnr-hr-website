HR Consulting

Strategic HR Support for Every Stage of Growth

Our HR consulting services are designed to help organisations navigate the complexities of South African labour law, build robust people management systems, and create workplaces where both employees and employers thrive.

Whether you need a full labour law audit, assistance with disciplinary matters, or ongoing HR partnership support, our team brings over 12 years of practical experience to every engagement.

“We work closely with our clients to understand their unique business goals, challenges, and culture. Through partnership and open communication, we provide customised HR solutions that support sustainable business growth.”

What We Cover
  • —Labour Law audits and compliance
  • —Design and implementation of company policies and procedures
  • —Drafting and management of Labour Law and employment-related documents
  • —Employment Equity Act management and reporting
  • —Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) and Annual Training Report (ATR) preparation
  • —Discretionary grant application and administration
  • —Coordination and management of employee training and development
  • —Recruitment and retention including workforce planning
  • —Performance management systems and process support
  • —Employee grievance resolution and case management
  • —Disciplinary and incapacity compliance management
  • —Representation in disciplinary enquiries
  • —Chairing of disciplinary enquiries
  • —Representation at labour tribunals
  • —Union relationship management and facilitation
  • —Plant-level employee negotiations
  • —Labour Law compliance training and coaching for management
  • —People management and leadership development coaching
  • —Guidance and support on best practice for both management and employees
  • —Payroll Management and Reporting
